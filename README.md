### Systems first.

**ALAPKO** takes its name from the Hungarian *alapkő* — **foundation stone**.

We build technology the same way: start with the foundation, understand the system, and engineer upward.

Our work spans infrastructure, automation, internal tooling, systems integration, observability, and practical AI-assisted operations — with an emphasis on software that is understandable, auditable, and built to survive outside the demo.

```text
FOUNDATION
    │
    ├── Infrastructure
    ├── Automation
    ├── Operations
    ├── Software
    ├── Integration
    └── Intelligence
           │
           └── reliable systems
```

### What we build

We work at the intersection of traditional infrastructure and modern software engineering:

- **Infrastructure engineering** — networks, systems, cloud, identity, and operations
- **Automation** — removing repetitive work without removing human oversight
- **Internal platforms** — purpose-built tools for real operational workflows
- **Systems integration** — connecting software, infrastructure, APIs, and services cleanly
- **Observability** — making complex environments easier to understand and operate
- **Applied AI** — bounded agents and AI-assisted workflows with explicit permissions and control

### Engineering philosophy

```text
Understand the boundary.
Define the contract.
Automate the repeatable.
Observe the result.
Keep a human in control.
```

We favor small interfaces, explicit permissions, deterministic behavior where it matters, strong audit trails, and systems that fail visibly rather than mysteriously.
